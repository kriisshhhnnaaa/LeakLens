Local-first sensitive-data workbench

Your next public paste shouldn't be a security incident.

LeakLens looks for the shapes credentials, tokens, private keys, and personal data take in text, and helps you redact them before you share the file. It runs entirely in this tab: your text never leaves your machine.

Document

What this tool is not: a fixed set of rules looks for common shapes — it cannot know your context, and it does not catch everything. A report of no findings is not proof the text is safe to share. Read it yourself.
